# Ingredients [for making]

## Oils

- 55 g Evening primrose oil
- 50 g Rose-hip oil
- 50 g Pomegranate seed oil
- 40 g Apricot oil
- 30 g Beeswax
- 20 g Shea butter

## Water

- 160 ml Aloe Vera gel
- 115 ml Rose water
- 15 ml Distilled water
- 3 ml Vit E

## Thickener

- 1% - 5 g Guar guar
- 4% 20 g Glycerine
