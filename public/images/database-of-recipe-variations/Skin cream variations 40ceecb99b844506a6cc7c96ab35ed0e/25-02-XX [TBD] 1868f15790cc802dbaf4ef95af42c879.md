# Ingredients [for making]

## Oils

- 100 g Coconut Oil (hydration and antibacterial properties)
- 80 g Apricot Oil (gentle and nourishing)
- 57  g Rose-hip Oil (rich in antioxidants and repairs skin)
- 3 g Pomegranate oil
- 100 g Shea Butter (moisturising and creamy texture)
- 60 g Beeswax (emulsifier and protective layer)

## Water

- 320 ml Aloe Vera Gel (hydration and soothing)
- 260 ml  Rosewater (calming and fragrant)
- 20 ml Distilled Water: l (balances texture)
- 6 ml Vitamin E Oil (antioxidant and preservative)

## Thickener

- 1% - 10 g Guar guar
- 4% - 40 g Glycerine
