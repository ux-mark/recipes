### Instructions:

1. **Phase 1: Oil Mixture**
    - In a heatproof container, combine Beeswax, Stearic Acid, Hydrogenated Castor Oil, and Cetearyl Olivate.
    - Melt the oils and waxes over a double boiler until fully liquefied.
2. **Phase 2: Water Mixture**
    - In a separate heatproof container, mix Water, Glycerin, and Rice Extract.
    - Slowly heat this mixture to approximately 70°C (158°F).
3. **Combine Phases**
    - Mix the Argaon oil into a bowl and add the warmer oils.
    - Gradually add the water phase to the melted oils while blending with an immersion blender or hand mixer until emulsified.
4. **Add Dry Ingredients**
    - Sift in Kaolin Clay and Arrowroot Powder. Blend well to ensure no lumps.
5. **Preservatives & pH Balancing**
    - Allow the mixture to cool to 40°C (104°F) before stirring in Potassium Sorbate, Citric Acid, and Glucono-Delta-Lactone.
6. **Final Adjustments**
    - Test the pH of the pomade to ensure it's between 4.5 and 5.5. Adjust with a tiny amount of Citric Acid if needed.
7. **Storage**
    - Transfer to a sterilised jar or tin and allow to cool completely. Store in a cool, dry place.
