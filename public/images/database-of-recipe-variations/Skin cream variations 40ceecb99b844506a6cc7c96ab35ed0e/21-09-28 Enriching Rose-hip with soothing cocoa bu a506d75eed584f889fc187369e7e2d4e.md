# Ingredients [for making]:

## Oils

- 100 ml Apricot oil
- 55 g Cocoa butter
- 55 g Coconut oil
- 30 g Beeswax

## Water

- 160 ml Aloe Vera gel
- 120 ml Rosewater
- 10 ml Colloidal silver water
- 3 ml Vit E oil

## Fragrance

- 20 drops Geranium essential oil
