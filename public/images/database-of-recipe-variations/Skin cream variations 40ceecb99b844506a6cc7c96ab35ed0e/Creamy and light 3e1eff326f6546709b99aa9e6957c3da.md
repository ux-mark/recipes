## Ingredients

- 35g beeswax
- 90g Coconut oil
- 50g Almond kernel oil
- 25g Rosehip oil
- 80g Aloe Vera gel
- 15g Glycerin
- 10g Water
- 5g Vitamin E
- 25g Honey
- 25g white clay
- 10 drops essential oil (peppermint is good)
